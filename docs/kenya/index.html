What is included, and what these counts mean

Kenyan sources mentioning China

These 17 websites are assigned Kenya by the existing GDELT-based origin lookup and country-domain clues. They are candidate sources, not a verified census of Kenyan news organizations. Each included URL has a Chinese geographic mention in GDELT’s V2Locations field; Chinese cities and regions also qualify.

Geography is a filter

A mention does not prove that China is the story’s main subject. A Kenyan location can be a dateline. The “only pair” filter excludes URLs with any other detected geographic country code, including separately coded territories. Countries that GDELT missed cannot be counted.

Dates and article labels

Dates show when GDELT observed the URL, not verified publication dates. The sample includes some older pages. Most link labels are derived from URL paths and are explicitly marked; they are not verified headlines. The 12 reviewed examples have retrieved page titles and assistant assessments from September 2026.

Reading the reviews

Review notes assess the retrieved article body, with one local article limited to a paywalled preview. Current pages may differ from the 2025 version. These examples are not a random sample or an accuracy estimate. Local publication does not establish local authorship or audience exposure.
