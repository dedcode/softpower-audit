How to read this audit Definitions, source gaps & limitations

What is being counted?

One daily record counts distinct article URLs within one outlet and country pair. A URL seen on different days can count on each day. Weekly, monthly and yearly sums are daily URL observations, not distinct articles over those longer periods. Website counts use distinct registered domains: subdomains are grouped together. Each website is counted once across the selected date range, and once within each interval in the time breakdown.

What does “local” mean?

Local means the estimated publisher country matches the target. Chinese means the estimate is China. Third country means another known country. This says where the publishing outlet is provisionally assigned, not who wrote a syndicated article or which audience read it.

How are countries assigned?

GDELT's 2015–2021 coverage-based estimate is compared with country-domain clues. Disagreements remain uncertain. Agreement is not proof of headquarters or ownership. Domains can be used internationally, and historical outlets may have changed location.

What does this audit establish?

It helps select pairs and periods with enough usable source coverage. Chinese publishing origin does not establish a soft-power campaign. Local reporting is not a measurement of public opinion. Identifying strategy and audience response needs further evidence.

How do stories enter a pair?

Chinese-classified outlets contribute target countries mentioned in GDELT's extracted locations. Other known outlets contribute their own country when China is mentioned, plus countries mentioned alongside China. Uncertain outlets require both China and the target. The same story can contribute to multiple pairs.

Missing dates and incomplete periods

Missing source days are marked, never silently treated as zero coverage. The research-criteria check excludes periods containing missing source days. Periods at the edges are evaluated only over the selected dates. Days use GDELT's UTC observation date, not a verified publication date.
