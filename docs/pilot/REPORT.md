# China–Kenya geography-filter pilot

January 2025 GDELT observation dates. All 4,122 candidates retained; no row limit. Broad daily counts match the existing extracted table for every website. Distinct monthly URL counts and daily observations happen to be identical in this month.

| Filter | Local URLs | Local websites | Chinese-classified URLs | Chinese-classified websites |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Broad candidates | 429 | 17 | 1114 | 157 |
| Both countries | 178 | 16 | 870 | 136 |
| Only China and Kenya | 54 | 9 | 69 | 28 |
| Only pair + Kenyan place | 39 | 7 | 39 | 8 |

## Findings

- The strictest filter leaves 39 URLs from 7 local websites and 39 URLs from 8 Chinese-classified websites. Equal URL counts do not imply equal diversity or independent reporting.
- Strict filters retain useful examples: Chinese retail expansion, Kenyan battery recycling using Chinese technology, and an embassy/media-group cultural event in Nairobi.
- They also retain noise: an internet-safety comparison and a health story with a Nairobi dateline. Country tags can disagree with the retrieved article body.
- Excluding every third country loses relevant material: Chinese development narratives quoting Kenyan experts, and wider trade reporting with a concrete Kenyan business connection.
- A place-name requirement can also miss relevant articles: a Xinhua photo story names Nairobi in its captions, but its saved location entries are country-level only.
- Requiring both countries also loses locally published coverage about China itself: a BBC-attributed story in The Star describes Chinese graduate unemployment without a Kenyan geographic mention.
- January here means GDELT observation date. Two sampled pages display publication dates in 2014 and 2015. Publication timing needs a separate check before studying change over time.

## Review scope

24 examples selected across 2 publisher groups × 4 disjoint geography categories, with 3 different websites per category. 23 article bodies read and 1 paywalled preview; no unresolved body-access failures after extraction repair. Deterministic, website-spread selection; not a random sample or a precision estimate. Judgments are assistant assessments of pages retrieved on 20 September 2026, not archived 2025 text.

Keep the geography flags as selectable audit views. Do not use only-pair or specific-place flags as the final relevance label. Separately assess article text, publisher/content origin and publication date before scaling a strategy analysis.

## Reproducibility

Main query processed 5,118,847,714 bytes; billed 5,119,148,032 bytes. At the previously used $6.25/TiB reference rate, this is approximately $0.029, before free allowances. This is arithmetic at a reference rate, not a billing statement.

Run `python3 analysis/run_kenya_pair_pilot.py`. Saved inputs are reused on subsequent runs. `raw_daily.jsonl` preserves full V2Locations strings; `articles.jsonl` preserves merged monthly flags; CSV exports and `query.sql` accompany this report. For duplicate records, locations are combined before testing for extra countries.

Specific places are GDELT type 4 (city/landmark outside the US) or 5 (first-level administrative region), with country KE. [Official field documentation](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/google-bigquery-gkg-2-0-sample-queries/).

A URL is not necessarily a distinct story, a verified publication date, or evidence of audience exposure. Origin classifications are provisional. Monthly union-of-locations is conservative for updated pages. Historical GDELT text is unavailable here, so current-body mismatches are flagged without assuming their cause.
